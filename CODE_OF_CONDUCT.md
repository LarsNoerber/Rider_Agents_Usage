# Code of conduct

AgentMeter welcomes constructive participation in issues, pull requests, discussions and the Wiki.

- Treat people respectfully and discuss the work rather than attacking its author.
- Provide useful context, accept good-faith corrections and keep discussion relevant.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or deliberately disrupt other participants.
- Do not publish another person's private information, credentials or private conversations.

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation using
GitHub's moderation tools. For concerns, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile and ask for a private
channel without posting sensitive details. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
